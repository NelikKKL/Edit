codicon.ttf is a SUBSET (34 glyphs, listed in src/codicons.rs) of the Codicons icon font, built from the official
SVG sources and codepoints in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-codicons
(src/icons/*.svg, src/template/mapping.json). The upstream prebuilt
dist/codicon.ttf is not checked into that repo, so the subset was generated
with fontTools; glyph outlines are unchanged apart from being converted
from SVG paths to TrueType quadratic curves.
